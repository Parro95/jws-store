Quick answers before you buy

What's the first piece of home gym equipment to buy?

A bench — it unlocks pressing, rows, step-ups and more with any weights you own. A foldable bench-and-rack combo goes furthest if barbell training is the goal.

Doorway or wall-mounted pull-up bar?

Doorway for renters (no screws, up in seconds); wall-mount for permanence and heavier, more confident training. Both here are rated well beyond bodyweight.

Is rubber gym flooring necessary?

If you're dropping anything heavier than a yoga mat, yes — 15mm commercial tiles protect concrete, quieten the room, and stop bars and bells denting everything including themselves.
