Quick answers before you buy

Are smart scale body-fat readings accurate?

They're estimates from bioelectrical impedance — not lab-grade, but consistent. Weigh at the same time each day and the *trend* is very reliable, which is what matters for progress.

Eufy P2 or P2 Pro — which one?

The Pro adds heart-rate and a 16th measurement. If you just want weight + body composition tracked painlessly, the standard P2 does the job for less.

Do they work with Apple Health / Google Fit?

Yes — all the scales here sync automatically to their app, which pushes to Apple Health, Google Fit and Fitbit.
