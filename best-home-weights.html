Quick answers before you buy

Fixed or adjustable weights for home?

Adjustable, almost always — one 18kg adjustable kettlebell replaces four fixed bells' worth of space and money. Fixed weights only win when two people train together at different loads.

What kettlebell weight should I start with?

A common starting point many coaches suggest: around 8–12kg for most beginners, 12–16kg if you already train. Adjustable bells sidestep the question entirely — start light, dial up as you progress.

Is a barbell set worth it at home?

Once you have a bench or rack, yes — barbell lifts load the whole body in ways dumbbells can't match, and the plate sets here include the bar and collars.
