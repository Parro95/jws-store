This list is arranged in the order I'd genuinely hand them to a new trader in Australia: mindset first, then the technical foundations, then the perspective that keeps you humble. All six are available on Amazon AU, most under $60, and together they cover more than any $2,000 "trading course" I've seen.

1. Trading in the Zone — Mark Douglas

★★★★★ 4.7 on Amazon · Trading psychology · The all-time classic

If you only ever read one trading book, it's this one. Douglas's core argument — that consistent profits come from discipline and probabilistic thinking, not from predicting markets — is the single idea that separates traders who last from traders who don't. You'll re-read it every year and find something new. Start here before you touch a chart.

Beginner questions, quickly answered

Which trading book should I read first?

Trading in the Zone. Strategy means nothing without the discipline to follow it, and Douglas builds that foundation better than anyone. If you want something more hands-on alongside it, pair it with How to Day Trade for a Living.

Are these books relevant for Australian traders?

Yes — all six teach principles (psychology, risk, charting) that apply to any market, whether you trade ASX shares, US stocks, crypto or forex. All links above go to Amazon Australia with local delivery.

Do I need to read all six?

No. Read #1 and #2 and you're ahead of most beginners. Add Murphy (#4) when you're chart-curious, and the rest as your interest grows.
