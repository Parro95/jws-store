Quick answers before you buy

Massage gun or foam roller first?

Roller first if you're choosing one — it covers more muscle for less money. The gun earns its place for targeted deep work and pre-session activation once you're training hard.

What's a trigger point ball for?

Feet, glutes, forearms, chest — the small, stubborn spots a roller glides straight over. The MobiPoint's textured surface is physio-grade for exactly this.
